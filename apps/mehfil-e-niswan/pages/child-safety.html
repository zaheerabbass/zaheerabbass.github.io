Mehfil-e-Niswan Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Developer: Zee Aay Studio

Mehfil-e-Niswan is an Android app by Zee Aay Studio where women and girls share, without names, what they are cooking today. It is meant for people aged 13 and over. These standards explain how we deal with child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Zero tolerance

We do not allow any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, abuses or endangers children. This includes CSAM, grooming, sexual messages to or about children, and any attempt to contact a child for sexual purposes. Such content breaks our rules and is removed.

2. How the app limits risk

  • There are no private messages, profiles, followers or friend lists. Everything is posted in one public daily chat.
  • Posts are text and emoji only. Images, videos and links are not supported (a typed web address stays plain text and cannot be tapped).
  • Before posting, everyone must accept the app's rules, which forbid sexual content and any content that exploits or endangers children.
  • A word filter blocks posts that contain words on our blocked list.
  • Posts containing a Pakistani phone number, an email address or a CNIC (national ID card) number are refused, which makes it harder to move a conversation off the app.

3. How to report

  • In the app: long-press a post, tap the ⋮ menu at the top and choose "Delete aur report karein" (delete and report), then confirm. The post is removed from your phone and reported to us. In the same menu, "Poster ko block karein" (block the poster) hides that person's posts on your phone only. Our email address is also shown in the app, under "Qaide" and "Madad".
  • By email: zastudio.apps@gmail.com. Describe what you saw and roughly when. Do not send images of abuse.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police first (15 in Pakistan).

4. What we do

  • Posts reported by several people are removed automatically. We check reports and remove posts that break our rules.
  • When we find content that sexualises or endangers children, we remove it for everyone.
  • We report CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities as the law requires, including Pakistan's law enforcement authorities and, where it applies, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Posts and reports are kept on our server for 90 days and then deleted automatically (see our Privacy Policy). Within that time they can be given to the authorities if lawfully requested. We do not hold anyone's name or contact details.

5. Laws

We follow the child safety laws that apply to us, including the laws of Pakistan, and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

6. Child safety contact

Our point of contact for child safety: Zee Aay Studio, zastudio.apps@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
