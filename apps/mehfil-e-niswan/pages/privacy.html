Mehfil-e-Niswan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Developer: Zee Aay Studio

Mehfil-e-Niswan is an Android app where women and girls share, without names, what they are cooking today ("Aaj kya pakayen?"). This policy explains what information the app and its server keep, why, and for how long. The app is made by Zee Aay Studio ("we", "us").

In short: the app never asks for your name, phone number or email. You use it under an anonymous identity that is created on your phone. Your posts are public to everyone using the app that day, and they are kept on our server for 90 days, even after you delete them, so that abuse can be checked. The app shows Google AdMob ads, and Google collects some data for those ads.

1. Your anonymous identity

When you first open the app, it signs in to our server with an anonymous account. This account has a random identifier and no name, phone number, email address, password or profile. The app never asks you for these, and the server refuses to attach an email or phone number to the account.

The identity lives in the app's private storage on your phone. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, the next start creates a new identity, and the old one can no longer be used. Records stored under the old identity stay on the server until their deletion time below. The identity record itself (with the time it was created and last signed in, but no IP address), your blocks and your rules-acceptance record are kept indefinitely.

Posts are shown without any name or identifier. The server never sends anyone an identifier of who wrote a post. "Anonymous" here means we do not ask who you are; it does not mean nothing about your use can be recorded, so please read the rest of this policy.

3. Technical logs (IP addresses)

Our server runs on Supabase, a hosting provider. Like any internet service, it sees your IP address and basic connection details when the app connects.

  • We do not store the IP address or device/browser details on your sign-in sessions.
  • The sign-in audit log, which records IP addresses, is deleted after 7 days.
  • Supabase's own request logs, which also include IP addresses, are kept only for the short period set by its service, after which they are deleted automatically.

We use these logs only to run the service and protect it from abuse (for example, limiting how many new accounts one network can create).

4. What stays on your phone

These stay in the app's private storage and are not sent to us, except that a post's text is sent when you send it:

  • your unsent draft and the queue of posts waiting to be sent;
  • a copy of the feed, so it opens quickly and works offline;
  • which posts you have already seen;
  • your recent emoji and skin-tone choice.

This data is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfer.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only, with restricted data processing, and the app does not request the advertising-ID permission. On Android versions older than 13, the AdMob software may still read the device's advertising ID, which Google may use for purposes such as limiting how often an ad is shown and preventing fraud. The app sends AdMob nothing about you or your posts: no identity, no post text, no keywords.

The AdMob software in the app does collect some information for itself, such as your IP address, device and app information, and how ads are shown and tapped, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Google Play app check

The app does not currently use Google Play Integrity (a Google check that the app is a genuine copy). If we add it, we will update this policy before it starts.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, or store, your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, location or a password.
  • We do not sell your data. Apart from AdMob for ads (section 5), we do not use any third-party analytics or tracking service.
  • The app asks for no special phone permissions.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your own post at any time ("Sab ke liye delete karein"). It disappears from everyone's feed within moments (at their next refresh), but it is kept on the server for 90 days for moderation and then deleted.

Because we never know who you are, we cannot reliably tell which records are yours from an email or message. For that reason we cannot look up, export or delete a person's data on request; it is deleted on the schedule in section 2. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

9. Who can see your data

  • Other users see your posts (without any name or identifier) for the day they were posted.
  • Our moderators can see stored posts, reports and moderation records, to deal with abuse.
  • Service providers: Supabase (hosting) and Google (ads) process data as described above.
  • The law: we may share stored records if the law requires it. We do not hold your name or contact details, so we cannot share them.

10. Security

All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS). The app cannot read the server's tables directly; it can only use a small set of server functions that never return who wrote a post.

11. Age

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not use it. We do not allow content that sexualises or endangers children: report it in the app ("Report") or write to the contact address in section 13, and we will remove it and report it to the authorities where the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. A change to how long data is kept will be shown here before it takes effect where we can.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: zastudio.apps@gmail.com (Zee Aay Studio). Please do not send post text or personal details you do not want us to see.
